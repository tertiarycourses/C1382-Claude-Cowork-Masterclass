# Lab 01 checklist — Your First Live Artefact

- [ ] Open the Claude desktop app and sign in with your paid-plan account.
- [ ] Choose Cowork in the sidebar to open the agent workspace rather than a plain chat.
- [ ] Paste the lab prompt into the composer and send it.
- [ ] Try the converter: change the amount and switch the currencies.
- [ ] Ask for one refinement in plain English, for example: Add a thousands separator to the result.
- [ ] Save the artefact so it appears in your sidebar.
- [ ] Download a copy of the artefact to your lab folder.

## Done when

- [ ] Converting S$10,000 to USD shows US$7,400. The artefact appears in your sidebar after saving, still works when you reopen it, and a downloaded copy is in your lab folder.
