# Lab 02 checklist — Your First Live Artefact

- [ ] Start a new Cowork session — this lab needs no files.
- [ ] Paste the lab prompt into the composer and send it.
- [ ] Try the converter: change the amount and switch the currencies.
- [ ] Check the live rate against a search for "1 USD to SGD".
- [ ] Click Refresh, and note the 'last fetched' time updates.
- [ ] Ask for one refinement in plain English, for example: Add a thousands separator to the result.
- [ ] Save the artefact so it appears in your sidebar.
- [ ] Download a copy of the artefact to your lab folder.

## Done when

- [ ] Converting S$10,000 to USD gives a figure that matches a live search for the SGD/USD rate, and the artefact shows when the rates were last fetched. Clicking Refresh updates that time. The artefact appears in your sidebar after saving, still works when you reopen it, and a downloaded copy is in your lab folder.
