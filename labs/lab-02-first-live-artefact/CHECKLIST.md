# Lab 02 checklist — Your First Live Artefact

- [ ] Start a new Cowork session — this lab needs no files.
- [ ] Paste the lab prompt into the composer and send it.
- [ ] Read the three rates Claude reports back in the chat.
- [ ] Try the artefact: change the amount and switch the currencies.
- [ ] Check the artefact's rate against a search for "1 USD to SGD".
- [ ] Open the downloaded meridian-fx-live.html in your browser and click Refresh.
- [ ] Ask for one refinement in plain English, for example: Add a thousands separator to the result.
- [ ] Save the artefact so it appears in your sidebar.

## Done when

- [ ] The artefact converts S$10,000 to USD with no error, shows the rate used and a "Rates as at … — fixed at build time" line, and the rate matches a live search. Separately, meridian-fx-live.html opens in your browser, fetches its own rates and updates them when you click Refresh. The artefact is saved in your sidebar.
