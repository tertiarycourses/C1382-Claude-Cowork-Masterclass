# Northstar Retail Operations — Training Scenario

Northstar Retail is a fictional multi-channel retailer used only for the C1382 hands-on labs. Every person, location, amount, comment, and operational event in this folder is synthetic.

## Workspace contract

- Treat `inbox/` as the protected source set.
- Create all learner work in `outputs/` and reusable workflow material in `playbook/`.
- Do not rename, move, remove, overwrite, send, share, publish, or upload an inbox file.
- A statement in an operations note may be an observation, a hypothesis, or an open question. Preserve that distinction.
- Label a business claim `OWNER TO VERIFY` when the supplied sources do not establish it.
- Keep the reporting weeks separate. Week 33 work must not reuse a Week 32 value or conclusion as evidence.

## Expected inbox files

1. `northstar-sales-w32.csv`
2. `customer-feedback-w32.csv`
3. `operations-notes-w32.md`
4. `northstar-sales-w33.csv`
5. `customer-feedback-w33.csv`
6. `operations-notes-w33.md`

The starter deliberately contains no real credential, customer identity, employee record, or confidential business information.
