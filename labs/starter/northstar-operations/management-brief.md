# Northstar Weekly Operations Brief

## Audience and decision

The Head of Operations needs a weekly management pack that shows performance against the operating targets, identifies material location exceptions, connects quantitative signals with supplied notes and customer feedback, and proposes a small number of reviewable actions.

## Reporting targets

- Revenue: at least SGD 330,000 per week.
- On-time fulfilment: at least 95.00% of orders.
- Return rate: no more than 4.50% of orders.
- All overall rates must be weighted from total numerators and denominators. Do not average location percentages.
- Currency is Singapore dollars. Display whole-dollar revenue in executive views and two decimal places for revenue per order.
- Display rates to two decimal places.

## Required management pack

1. An analysis workbook with preserved raw data, metric definitions, formulas or transparent calculations, location detail, feedback themes, targets, and sources.
2. A written management report with an executive summary, KPI table, exceptions, evidence-based drivers, three prioritised recommendations, risks, owner questions, methodology, and sources.
3. A five-to-seven-slide leadership brief with a scorecard, exceptions, supporting evidence, proposed actions, and decisions or owner questions.
4. A source register, quality record, and run log that make the workflow reviewable.

## Evidence rules

- A number from a CSV is source evidence.
- A statement in an operations note keeps its original status: confirmed observation, hypothesis, or open question.
- A pattern across several supplied sources may be described as an inference and must name those sources.
- An unsupported cause, commitment, owner, date, or external fact is labelled `OWNER TO VERIFY`.
- Recommendations are proposals for human review, not automatic decisions.
