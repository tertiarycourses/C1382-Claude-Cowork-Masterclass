# Trainer Checkpoint: Weekly Operations Playbook Starter

Use this only if your own playbook has not reached its first review gate by the stated timebox. Adapt it into the required files; do not paste Week 32 values or conclusions into reusable rules.

## Required parameters

- `REPORTING_WEEK`
- `SOURCE_FOLDER`
- `OUTPUT_FOLDER`
- `AUDIENCE`
- `TARGET_SET`

## Source contract and stop conditions

Resolve the exact period-specific sales, feedback, and operations-note files plus the approved management brief and style guide. Stop for a missing, duplicate, stale, out-of-period, unapproved, or unreadable source. Treat source folders as read-only and write only to the parameterised output folder.

## Stage 1: parallel extraction and reconciliation

1. Data workstream: preserve raw rows, calculate defined metrics, weighted rates, controls, and target exceptions.
2. Evidence workstream: extract observations and themes with exact provenance; separate evidence, inference, and `OWNER TO VERIFY` items.
3. Source workstream: record every approved input, period, purpose, use, and limitation.

Merge the three workstreams into `source-register.md`, `evidence-register.md`, `operations-analysis.xlsx`, and `reconciliation.md`. Pause for a named reviewer to compare the artifacts with independent controls. Record the evidence, decision, time, and permitted next action.

## Stage 2: audience artifacts

After Stage 1 approval, create `management-report.docx`, `leadership-brief.pptx`, and `run-log.md` from the reconciled fact base. Pause again for visual, numerical, provenance, and cross-artifact review. Do not share, send, publish, replace a source, or declare release without explicit authority.

## Quality and recovery

Check source completeness and freshness, formula preservation, weighted rates, target exceptions, evidence labels, period labels, layout, cross-artifact consistency, unresolved questions, inbox hashes, and the release decision. On failure, record the observed and expected state, correct the smallest responsible stage, and repeat every affected downstream check.

## Ownership and version history

Name the workflow owner, source owner, reconciliation reviewer, artifact reviewer, and release owner. Record each material change with version, date, reason, evidence, and approver.
