# Meridian Capital Partners — Brand Guidelines (internal)

These are the house rules every Meridian report, dashboard and document follows.
In Lab 7 these rules go into the Claude Project as standing instructions, so
every chat in the project applies them without being told.

## Colours
| Use | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / headers | Deep navy | `#1B2A4A` |
| Accent / highlights | Gold | `#C6A664` |
| Background / panels | Light grey | `#F4F4F2` |

## Typography
- Headings: a serif typeface.
- Body text: a clean sans-serif typeface.

## Every output must carry
- The title **Meridian Capital Partners** in the header.
- The reporting period beside the title.
- The footer line: *Meridian Capital Partners Pte Ltd — internal use only*.

## Numbers
- All money in Singapore dollars, formatted `S$24,850,000`.
- Thousands separators always; no decimal places on whole amounts.
- Variances show both the absolute figure and the percentage, e.g. `+S$220,000 (+2.7%)`.

## Tone
Factual and concise. State the figure and the reason. No marketing language,
no superlatives, no exclamation marks.
