# Lab 03b — tools

This lab uses the following, all within Topic 01 (Claude Cowork Fundamentals):

- **Claude Cowork**
- **Live Artefacts**
- **local files**

No connectors are needed — the form and the output both stay on your own disk.

## Turning connectors on

Click the **+** in the chat composer, hover **Connectors**, and toggle on only
what this lab needs. You can manage them later under **Customize → Connectors**.

## Keep it scoped

Point Claude at the one folder or mailbox the task needs — never your whole
Drive or inbox. Claude asks for approval before it sends mail or changes a file;
read the draft and check the figures before you approve.

## What the artefact cannot do

An artefact is sandboxed: no filesystem, no network. It can collect and validate
your answers, but it cannot open `mock-data/claimants-statement-pa.pdf` or save
a filled copy. The Cowork session does that part.
