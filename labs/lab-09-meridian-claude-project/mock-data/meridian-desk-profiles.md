# Meridian Capital Partners — Desk Profiles (internal)

The four revenue desks, so Claude can write about any of them without being briefed each time. All figures are FY2025 and synthetic.

## Advisory

Buy-side and sell-side advisory on mid-market transactions across Singapore and Malaysia. The firm's largest desk by revenue and deal count. Headed by Priya Raman. FY2025 revenue S$8,420,000 across 64 deals, of which S$2,390,000 was booked in Q4.

## Asset Management

Discretionary portfolio management for institutional and family-office clients, with a Singapore real-asset bias. Headed by Daniel Ong. FY2025 revenue S$6,700,000 across 56 deals, of which S$1,880,000 was booked in Q4.

## Private Credit

Direct lending and structured credit to mid-market borrowers across the region, typically on three- to five-year terms. Headed by Clara Sim. FY2025 revenue S$5,330,000 across 50 deals, of which S$1,500,000 was booked in Q4.

## Corporate Finance

Capital raising, refinancing and restructuring mandates, including the firm's listed-company work. Headed by Marcus Tan. FY2025 revenue S$4,400,000 across 44 deals, of which S$1,210,000 was booked in Q4.

Firm total: S$24,850,000 across 214 deals, with S$6,980,000 in Q4.
