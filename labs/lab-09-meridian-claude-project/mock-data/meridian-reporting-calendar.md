# Meridian Capital Partners — Reporting Calendar (internal)

What finance produces, and when. Claude reads this so it knows what is due
without being told each time.

| Report | Cadence | Due | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily brief | Every weekday | 9:00am | Finance team |
| Desk review | Monthly | 3rd working day of the following month | Desk heads |
| Board pack | Quarterly | 10 working days after quarter end | Board |
| FY close | Annually | 20 working days after 31 December | Board and auditors |

## Notes
- The daily brief is drafted, never sent automatically.
- The quarterly board pack carries charts, not screenshots of charts.
- FY2025 is the current reporting year throughout.
